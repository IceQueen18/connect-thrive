Westside Senior Center - Attendance & Ride Log
Quick-start guide for staff and volunteers

Every shift:
1. Open the attendance log on the front-desk tablet and sign in with your
   center account.
2. Select today's program and record each participant with the large-text
   check-in buttons.
3. For rides, add the pickup time and driver name before 10 a.m.
4. At the end of the shift, tap "Done" so the coordinator can see totals.

Never enter participant addresses, health notes or emergency contact
details in the log. Keep entries to names and attendance only.

If the tablet will not connect, use the paper backup sheet at the desk and
tell the program coordinator the same day.
