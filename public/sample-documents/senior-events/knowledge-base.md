# Westside Senior Center knowledge base

## Quick start
Sign in on the front-desk tablet, choose today's program, and check
participants in with the large-text buttons. Record ride requests before
10 a.m. so drivers can plan their routes.

## Frequently asked questions
**Who can edit the attendance log?** Staff and scheduled volunteers with a
center account. The program coordinator approves new accounts.
**A participant was checked in twice.** Flag both records for the
coordinator; do not delete the record yourself.
**Where do last month's totals come from?** The coordinator opens the
monthly summary; volunteers do not need to calculate anything.

## Troubleshooting
**Tablet offline:** use the paper backup sheet and tell the coordinator the
same day. **Wrong program open:** check the date in the corner before
recording anything.
