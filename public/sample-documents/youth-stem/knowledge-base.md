## Quick start
Sign in with an organizational account and open the stem attendance and transport coordination workspace. Use the queue assigned to your role.

## Routine task
Generate a dated roster using participant codes, pickup groups and assigned staff. Do not include guardian contact details in shared exports.

## Access problems
Ask the operations coordinator to review your assigned role. Never share passwords or bypass access restrictions.

## Incorrect or duplicate data
Flag the entry for coordinator review. Preserve the source record and log the correction reason; do not delete records to hide an error.

## Service interruption
Test 35 synthetic participants, including duplicate attendance and pending consent. If exports fail, revert to the last validated synthetic roster and record the exception.

## Safe support request
Include the time, affected step and a sanitized error message. Do not send beneficiary data, passwords or screenshots of confidential records.

## Maintenance
The coordinator reviews access monthly, checks backup status weekly and schedules a restore drill. These are example tasks, not verified operational practices.
